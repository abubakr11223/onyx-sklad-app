## 4. Photo durability — investigated, no action taken

The two audits appeared to contradict each other. Both were partly right: storage
is **hybrid**, and `Photo.storageKey` holds either kind of reference.

- **Telegram `file_id`**: warehouse SLAB photos, bot SAMPLE, `/singan` PIECE
  (`telegram-webhook.ts:590-591,709-711,766-769`; `singan/actions.ts:137-144`)
- **Vercel Blob HTTPS URL**: intake pattern SAMPLE, `INTERIOR_AI`
  (`photo-blob.ts:45-50`; `priemka/actions.ts:252-271`; `kamen/actions.ts:571-638`)

`api/photo/[id]/route.ts` reads both: HTTPS → 308 redirect, otherwise `getFile` +
stream. So the photos that matter most for §5.3 «навсегда» — premium slab photos —
have a durable DB row pointing at bytes Telegram holds.

**Recommendation: no emergency rewrite.** Telegram `file_id` is officially
persistent; the risk was overstated. Open product question for the owner:

> Do premium slab photos stay on Telegram, or must every sales photo be copied
> into our own Blob storage?

If the answer is "copy them", the forward path (write new SLAB/PIECE photos to
Blob) is the smallest high-value change, with incremental backfill after.
