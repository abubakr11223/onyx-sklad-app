## 2. Deliberately not built — do not reopen without new information

**Offline write queue for sales and reservations.** Queuing them delays a
confirmation the spec makes exclusive: a manager tells a client the stone is held
for three days, the request lands two hours later, and the hold starts then. The
conditional updates stop a double sale; nothing stops the false promise. The
existing "needs network" banner is the honest behaviour. If an outbox is ever
required, locations and `needsCheck` are the safe candidates — reversible, no
exclusive claim. **Open question for the owner: is offline selling required for
go-live?** If not, this leaves the critical path entirely.

**Owner reporting product.** The main spec says «отчёты» once, as a single word,
defining no view. Valuation and dead-stock figures derive from computed remainders
(ADR-005) with purchase prices only partly filled — a report that looks
authoritative and is quietly wrong is worse than none. Revisit after real stock
and a few weeks of real sales; start with a period sales list.

## 3. Architectural constraints pinned by tests — do not "optimise" these away

- Availability **cannot** be pushed into SQL as a per-batch `EXISTS`:
  `freeRemainderFromAggregate` has no zero clamp, so `{+2,−3}` sums to −1 while
  `EXISTS` returns true, and no CHECK forbids negative free remainders.
- «Ничего не найдено.» and «Показать ещё» must never render together.
- Stock leaves availability at the moment of sale, sample issue, or showroom
  send — never at warehouse confirmation. Deferring it reopens the double-sale
  race the conditional `updateMany` guards close. ТЗ №15 §6 says this itself.
- A Sample has exactly one writer: `issueSample`. Warehouse confirmation records
  the physical hand-over and never creates or reactivates one.
- UZS and USD are never summed. There is no exchange rate in this system.
- Pagination uses a compound keyset. A timestamp-only cursor skips rows that
  share a timestamp — fixed twice, in sale history and in debts.
- Dimensions are stored and displayed in **centimetres** since the 2026-08-07
  migration divided the stored values. Columns keep their `*Mm` names; the
  cutting margin is 2 (cm). Re-running that division would destroy the warehouse.
